MICCAI 2026

BioFlow: A Biologically Valid Support-Preserving Flow for Histology-Conditioned Spatial Transcriptomics Prediction

Haoran Xu, Yang Liu, Wei Yuan, Xiao Han*

Identified that standard diffusion and flow trajectories leave the valid support of sparse, non-negative gene-expression data. Developed a support-preserving flow formulation that improved PCC by 2750% and reduced training time by 5200% over matched baselines.
