About

I am Haoran (Haron) Xu, interested in principled generative modeling when data are scarce but structure is rich. My work asks whether trajectory-based assumptions are always necessary, and explores alternatives that better align model design with underlying structure.

Research Interests

Foundations of Generative Modeling

Theoretical and conceptual questions about what makes generative models robust, interpretable, and data-efficient under constraints.

Structured, Low-Data Regimes

Learning with limited samples by explicitly leveraging latent structure, compositionality, and inductive bias.

Beyond Trajectory-Based Paradigms

Re-evaluating trajectory-centric assumptions and identifying cases where they are suboptimal for inference or generation.

Structure-Aligned Alternatives

Designing alternatives that align model mechanics with known structure to improve sample efficiency and reliability.
