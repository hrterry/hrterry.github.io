About

Research focus

Generative learning for biology

My research aims to close the gap between methods developed for natural image-text data and the distinct statistical, structural, and scientific constraints of biological data. I study generative objectives, post-training algorithms, and representation learning across biological sequences and multimodal spatial data.

Research Interests

Questions and directions

Direction 01

Generative Models

In generative modeling, I study how noise injection and denoising can induce useful generalization, and how scalable generative methods capture complex underlying data distributions.

Direction 02

Post-training

I study how post-training objectives and reward signals improve generation quality, model behavior, and generalization in structured scientific settings.

Direction 03

Foundation Models

I am interested in foundation models for biological sequences and multimodal data, especially their representations, generation behavior, and scientific usefulness.
