About

A short scholarly introduction

Biography

I am an undergraduate student at Sichuan University from 2023 to 2027. My research centers on scalable generative methods, multimodal learning, and biomedical AI, with a particular interest in building models that support scientific understanding rather than superficial prediction.

Current Focus

I am currently exploring scalable generative methods for biology and pathology, especially model designs that bridge heterogeneous modalities and produce useful representations for downstream scientific tasks.

Research Interests

Questions and directions

Direction 01

Generative Modeling

In generative modeling, I study how noise injection and denoising can induce useful generalization, and how scalable generative methods capture complex underlying data distributions.

Direction 02

Multimodal Learning

In multimodal learning, I study how scalable generative methods enable meaningful information exchange across modalities, and whether models learn useful signals rather than superficial correlations or shortcut patterns.

Direction 03

Reinforcement Learning

In reinforcement learning, I am interested in diffusion-based approaches whose stronger representations may improve the generalization and test-time scalability of SDE- and ODE-based methods.
