1f:Ta19,<p>The new ChatGPT Office add-in makes PowerPoint feel less like a blank canvas and more like a place where a draft can start moving quickly: install the add-in, open the ChatGPT sidebar, sign in, and ask it to create, edit, understand, or polish slides in the deck itself.</p>
<h2 id="prepare-microsoft-365">Prepare Microsoft 365</h2>
<p>If Microsoft 365 is not installed yet, this cnblogs post is a useful reference for the Office Tool Plus deployment flow: <a href="https://www.cnblogs.com/partyL/articles/18944070">Microsoft 365 installation notes</a>.</p>
<p>One practical caveat: for a machine you rely on, use a legitimate Microsoft subscription, school or company license, family sharing, or another properly authorized route. Third-party activation tools and KMS servers can create compliance, security, and reliability problems, so I would not treat them as the foundation for a long-term work setup.</p>
<h2 id="install-chatgpt">Install ChatGPT</h2>
<p>Install the <a href="https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/office/WA200010215?src=office&#x26;corrid=09f7c032-dbd8-dd85-a61b-acb63f0c4729&#x26;omexanonuid=&#x26;referralurl=">ChatGPT Office add-in from Microsoft Marketplace</a>. The same add-in can also be found from inside PowerPoint: open <code>Home</code>, choose <code>Add-ins</code>, search for <code>ChatGPT</code>, and add it.</p>
<p>After installation, reopen or bring up PowerPoint and launch ChatGPT from the ribbon. When the sidebar appears, sign in with the OpenAI / ChatGPT account that has access, then start working directly inside the presentation.</p>
<h2 id="useful-first-prompts">Useful first prompts</h2>
<ul>
<li>Turn a memo, abstract, or meeting note into a short slide deck.</li>
<li>Add a background, risks, or summary slide to an existing deck.</li>
<li>Review the story, gaps, and audience fit of the presentation.</li>
<li>Polish titles and bullets while preserving the current template.</li>
</ul>
<p>The final review still matters. ChatGPT for PowerPoint is currently a beta experience, and generated slides or edits may need manual cleanup. Check facts, numbers, citations, and formatting before sharing the deck.</p>
