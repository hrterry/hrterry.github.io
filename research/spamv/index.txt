1c:T515,<h2 id="interpretable-spatial-multi-omics-integration">Interpretable Spatial Multi-Omics Integration</h2>
<p>Different spatial omics modalities observe complementary aspects of the same tissue. Mapping all measurements into a single latent space can conflate what is shared across modalities with signals that are meaningful only within one modality.</p>
<h3 id="spamv">SpaMV</h3>
<p>SpaMV uses a multi-view representation-learning framework to separate <strong>shared latent structure</strong> from <strong>omics-specific private structure</strong>. This decomposition supports spatial domain analysis while retaining interpretable molecular programs that would otherwise be lost in a single shared embedding.</p>
<h3 id="research-role">Research Role</h3>
<p>This work forms an early foundation for my interest in multimodal biological representation learning: how models can connect modalities without erasing what makes each modality scientifically distinct.</p>
