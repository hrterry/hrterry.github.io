22:T544,<h2 id="when-biological-data-do-not-live-in-euclidean-space">When Biological Data Do Not Live in Euclidean Space</h2>
<p>Spatial transcriptomics prediction from histology is commonly formulated with diffusion or flow-matching models. Standard generative trajectories, however, evolve in unconstrained real-valued space and can produce negative gene-expression values that are biologically invalid.</p>
<h3 id="bioflow">BioFlow</h3>
<p>BioFlow incorporates non-negative support directly into the generative dynamics. A reparameterized velocity field preserves valid expression values throughout the probability path instead of repairing invalid outputs afterward.</p>
<h3 id="why-it-matters">Why It Matters</h3>
<p>The project asks a broader modeling question: if scientific observations live on a constrained space, should those constraints be part of the model's geometry? Encoding valid support into the generation process improves both scientific consistency and computational efficiency.</p>
